Hydraulic Hinge

A self-closing door hinge that holds the door open past 85° and closes it gently and fully below that, with closing speed and force you can adjust.

Request a quote See specifications

Features

  • Holds the door open automatically beyond 85°, with adjustable closing and latching speeds
  • Closes automatically from a maximum opening angle of 170°
  • Protects the door and the lock from impact damage when closing
  • Easy speed and force adjustment
  • Slim, elegant design
